Specification & Logistics Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

We carry artisanal wall formats from 100×100 mm and 300×600 mm, popular rectified floor formats in 600×600 mm, 600×1200 mm, and 800×1600 mm, timber planks in 200×1200 mm, outdoor pavers in 16–20 mm thickness, and sintered slabs in 1200×2400 mm, 1200×2800 mm, and 1600×3200 mm (6 mm, 9 mm, and 12 mm).

Over 85% of our catalogue is held in ready single-lot stock at our 65,000 sq. ft. climate-secure logistics hub for 48–72 hour dispatch. Bespoke Italian bookmatched slabs and special PVD finishes carry a 3–5 week scheduled lead time.

Walk-in visitors are welcome daily during opening hours. However, if you are selecting surfaces for multiple rooms or bringing architectural drawings, booking an appointment reserves a dedicated stylist and private sample table.

Large-format slabs travel upright on steel A-frames inside air-ride trucks. Any crate documented with transit damage upon unloading is replaced free of charge within 48 hours from the exact same shade lot.

We supply certified C2TE-S1/S2 polymer adhesives, levelling clips, and colour-matched epoxy grouts, plus a complimentary technical site briefing for your contractor—or introductions to our vetted master slab-laying teams.

Yes. Our Trade & Project Division offers tiered volume BOQ pricing, shade-lot reservation for up to 9 months, and floor-by-floor scheduled site deliveries for multi-unit developments.

Standard enquiries receive a formal itemised PDF quotation (including recommended 7–10% cutting wastage, box rounding, and GST breakdown) within 4 business hours.
