The Aurelia Standard

Why Discerning Projects Choose Our Showroom

More than a retail store—an architectural specification gallery engineered to remove guesswork from material selection.

  1. 01

    Certified Grade-A Quality

    Every batch is ISO 13006 & EN 14411 certified first-choice calibre—checked for planar flatness, zero warpage, and <0.05% water absorption.

  2. 02

    Exclusive Architectural Designs

    Direct kiln partnerships in Modena, Castellón, and Morbi bring exclusive vein-cut, fluted, and PVD brass finishes unavailable in mass outlets.

  3. 03

    Expert Surface & Bath Guidance

    In-house interior architects calculate exact tile wastage, adhesive grades, epoxy grout colour codes, and concealed valve compatibility.

  4. 04

    Unmatched Full-Slab Selection

    Inspect full-height 1600×3200 mm sliding slab libraries and 38 working water-tested shower & faucet displays under tunable 2700K–5000K lighting.

  5. 05

    Single-Lot Delivery & Support

    Guaranteed shade-lot reservation for phased residential builds, A-frame crane slab delivery, and rapid replacement insurance against transit breakage.
