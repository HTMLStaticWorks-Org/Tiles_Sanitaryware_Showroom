18 Years of Surface Curation
Our Story

From Boutique Tile Studio to 28,000 Sq. Ft. Flagship

Founded in 2008 by a duo of architectural material engineers, Aurelia began with a singular conviction: choosing tiles and bathware should feel like visiting a calm design museum—never a crowded warehouse of mismatched racks.

Over nearly two decades, we forged direct provenance agreements with premier sintering kilns and solid-brass PVD foundries, inspecting planar calibration, glaze depth, and water conservation before a single SKU earns a place on our gallery walls.

2008 — Origin

Opened our first 3,500 sq. ft. Italian porcelain studio.

2015 — Bath Atelier

Introduced PVD brassware, rimless WCs & stone resin vessels.

2021 — Slab Atrium

Inaugurated our 1600×3200 mm vertical monolith gallery.

2026 — Lighting Lab

Launched CRI-97 tunable daylight & trade specification suites.
